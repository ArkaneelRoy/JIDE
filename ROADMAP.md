## 🎯 The Mission
Our mission is simple: **Eliminate the need for a laptop.** Whether you're on a train, at a cafe, or just away from your desk, AndroidIDE Ultra should provide every tool necessary to build, test, and deploy professional-grade applications.

---

## 🛠️ The "Big Three" Pillars

### 1. The Visual Revolution (Preview System)
The community deserves a preview system that isn't just a placeholder, but a production tool.
- **Jetpack Compose Native Preview**: See your UI as it will actually look on the device.
- **Live Edit**: Code on the left, see updates on the right—instantly.
- **Visual Layout Inspector**: Debug your view hierarchy with a touch.

### 2. The Intelligence Layer (Claude Code)
We are integrating **Claude Code** to turn AIDEU into an AI-augmented powerhouse.
- **Autonomous Bug Fixing**: "Claude, find the memory leak in my login flow."
- **Natural Language Refactoring**: "Claude, convert this Activity to a Composable."
- **Context-Aware Docs**: Instant answers based on *your* specific project structure.

### 3. The Cross-Platform Expansion (Flutter)
Native Android is just the beginning.
- **Full Flutter/Dart Support**: Hot Reload on your phone.
- **Pub.dev Browser**: Add dependencies with a single tap.
- **Platform-Specific Previews**: Toggle between Android and iOS-style previews (UI only).
